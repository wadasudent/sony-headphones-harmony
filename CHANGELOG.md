# Changes / 更新记录

## 0.5.0

### English

- WH-1000XM6 controls include noise canceling and ambient sound mode selection, a 10-band EQ, and battery, firmware, and codec readings.
- The app can automatically pick up an already connected Sony headset on launch. Home screen widgets show battery and listening mode while connected and hide readings after disconnect.
- Left earbud, right earbud, and case battery display added for supported MDR V2 headsets. WF-1000XM5 readings were verified by packet replay. HarmonyOS hardware testing is pending.
- Phone and tablet layouts support light, dark, or system appearance and four accent colors.

### 简体中文

- WH-1000XM6 可切换降噪与环境声、调整十段均衡器，并查看电量、固件版本和当前编码。
- 打开应用时可自动接入系统已连接的索尼耳机。桌面卡片显示电量和聆听模式，断链后隐藏读数。
- 支持上报分耳电量的 MDR V2 耳机可分别显示左耳、右耳和充电盒电量。WF-1000XM5 已验证报文解析，鸿蒙真机待测。
- 手机和平板界面提供浅色、深色、跟随系统三种模式和四种强调色。
